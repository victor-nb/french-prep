# Quebec's PEQ Reopening — Definitive Research Report
**Announcement of June 10, 2026 | Report compiled June 10, 2026 (evening)**

---

## 1. TL;DR

1. **The PEQ is back — temporarily.** On Wednesday, June 10, 2026, Immigration Minister François Bonnardel announced the Programme de l'expérience québécoise (PEQ) will be reactivated for two years, **July 2, 2026 to July 2, 2028**, in both streams (Diplômés du Québec and Travailleurs étrangers temporaires), after which it is slated to close permanently in favour of the PSTQ. **[official]** (quebec.ca communiqué 71021 + program pages, all dated June 10, 2026)
2. **First application window: July 2 – October 31, 2026**, with the Arrima platform opening at **8:30 a.m. Montreal time on July 2**. **[official]**
3. **Grandfathering rule:** the first window is reserved for people who **already held an eligible Quebec diploma or had accumulated eligible work experience as of November 19, 2025** — the date the PEQ was abolished. **[official]**
4. **No cap on applications** in the first window: "Il n'y a pas de plafond" — every conforming application received will be processed. But **admissions** still fit inside the ~29,000/year economic-immigration envelope (~14,500/year via PEQ targeted), and the old 6-month processing commitment is **not renewed**. **[official]** (no-cap and 6-month withdrawal are in the communiqué; the 14,500 split is **[reported]**, minister's verbal statement)
5. **French requirements (key for test-takers):** oral **level 7** on the Échelle québécoise (≈ B2) for principal applicants in **both** streams; graduates additionally need **written level 5** (≈ B1); accompanying spouse needs **oral level 4** (≈ high A2). **[official]** On TCF Canada, level 7 oral means **≥ 400/699 in compréhension orale AND ≥ 10/20 in expression orale** (per the official MIFI correspondence table — pre-existing, not re-published for the PEQ). **[official table / unconfirmed carry-over]**
6. **Full program details and the NEW application forms arrive June 17, 2026**; old forms (A-0520-GF / A-0520-IF) will make an application non-receivable. **[official]**
7. **The ministry expects 8,000–12,000 (possibly ~14,000) first-window applicants** — far below the 125,000 figure floated during the CAQ leadership race, a gap Bonnardel could not explain ("C'était des prévisions à très haut niveau"). **[reported]** (press conference figure; appears in no official written document)
8. **No legal instrument exists yet.** As of June 10 evening, no regulation, arrêté or Gazette officielle publication implements the reopening — verified by full-text search of Gazette Partie 2 No. 23 (June 10) and the MIFI decisions page (frozen at May 19, 2026). The reopening currently rests on web pages and communiqués alone. **[official negative finding]**

## 4. Requirements

The MIFI explicitly distinguishes **critères de réception** (who may file during the window) from **conditions de sélection** (what must be met to get a CSQ): *"a person could meet the reception criteria to submit an application without meeting all the selection conditions, which would lead to a refusal."* **[official]** Selection conditions are stated to be **unchanged**: *"Les conditions de sélection du PEQ restent les mêmes qu'au moment de sa suspension pour le volet diplômés et lors de l'abolition du volet travailleurs."* **[official]**

### 4.1 Volet Diplômés du Québec (Quebec Graduates)

**Reception criteria (first window):** **[official]**
- Have obtained **in Quebec, as of November 19, 2025**, one of these diplomas: **baccalauréat, maîtrise, doctorat, DEC technique, DEP of 1,800+ hours, or DEP followed by an ASP totalling 1,800+ hours**.
- **Not eligible** (per the pre-suspension form, expected to carry over): AEP, AEC, DESS, postdoctorate, DES, general DEC, university certificates. **[official pre-suspension / unconfirmed for reopened program]**

**Selection conditions (as listed in communiqué 71021):** **[official]**
1. Be staying in Quebec ("séjourner au Québec") at the time of application.
2. Have stayed temporarily in Quebec mainly to study, for at least half the program's duration.
3. Diploma obtained **within the three years preceding the application** (anchored at application date — combined with the Nov 19, 2025 ceiling, a first-window applicant needs a diploma dated roughly between mid/late 2023 and Nov 19, 2025; pre-mid-2023 graduates appear excluded as written, with no accommodation mentioned). **[official text; the exclusion effect is analysis]**
4. French: **written level 5 AND oral level 7** (see §5).
5. No scholarship with a return-home condition (unless fulfilled).
6. Common conditions: intention to settle in Quebec to hold a job; demonstrated learning of democratic and Quebec values (also spouse and adult dependants); financial self-sufficiency commitment.

**What changed vs pre-suspension rules:**
- **No change announced** — conditions are explicitly those in force at suspension. **[official]**
- Reminder of the big *earlier* change that carries over: since **November 23, 2023**, graduates need **no work experience and no job** at application (only the *intention* to work). Reference sites still describing 12–18 months of required experience (e.g., Canadavisa) are **outdated**. **[official]**
- **In limbo:** the **French-of-studies condition** (program completed in French — operationalized as **≥75% of courses/credits in French** — OR 3+ years of full-time secondary/post-secondary study in French, in force since Nov 23, 2024) is **absent from the June 10 condition lists**, which instead list the written-level-5 test requirement. Because the lists are demonstrably simplified (they also omit passport/age-18 conditions) and MIFI says conditions are unchanged, the condition **probably carries over but is officially unconfirmed** until June 17. **[unconfirmed]**

### 4.2 Volet Travailleurs étrangers temporaires (Temporary Foreign Workers)

**Reception criteria (first window):** **[official]**
- **As of November 19, 2025**, have acquired in Quebec eligible work experience of **at least two years** in an occupation of category **FÉER (TEER) 0, 1, 2 or 3** of the CNP/NOC.
- ⚠️ **Lookback-window ambiguity:** the official texts say only *"d'une durée d'au moins deux ans, en date du 19 novembre 2025"* — **no 3-year window is stated**. La Presse and Moving2Canada both add "**within the previous three years**" anchored at Nov 19, 2025 (≈ Nov 19, 2022 – Nov 19, 2025). The pre-suspension regulation (RIQ art. 35) anchored a 2-of-3-years window **at the application date**. Which anchor governs is **unresolved** pending the legal text/forms. **[official text vs reported gloss — flag]**

**Selection conditions (as listed June 10):** **[official]**
1. **Occupy an eligible job at the time of application** — people who lost their job or left Quebec during the freeze would fail this condition as announced; no remedy mentioned.
2. French: **oral level 7** (no written requirement for workers).
3. Common conditions (intention to settle and work; values; self-sufficiency).

**Carried over from pre-abolition rules (form A-0520-IF, 2024-05 — expected but not re-published):** **[official pre-suspension / unconfirmed carry-over]**
- Full-time = paid work of **minimum 30 h/week**; 24 of the 36 months preceding the application in FÉER 0–3.
- Job/experience must **not** be for a business the applicant legally or de facto controls, nor in an **Annexe E ineligible sector** (payday loans/cheque cashing/pawnbroking; pornography/sex-industry services — disqualifying if the *employer* operates in the sector, whether or not the job contributes to it).
- Legal stay in Quebec with main purpose of working (or youth-exchange permits: Vacances-travail, Jeunes professionnels, Stage coop international).
- A **grandfathered variant** existed for people authorized to work in Quebec **before July 22, 2020** (12 of 24 months, no FÉER restriction) — its carry-over is **unconfirmed**.

**What changed vs pre-suspension rules:**
- The **Nov 19, 2025 anchor** for the experience criterion is new (the old rule was anchored at the application date). **[official]**
- A planned written-French requirement for workers (from Nov 23, 2024) does **not** appear in the announced conditions — workers need oral 7 only. **[official]**

### 4.3 Common conditions, both streams **[official, incl. pre-suspension forms]**
- Intention to settle in Quebec to hold a job.
- **Values attestation** (democratic and Quebec values, Charte des droits et libertés) — also required of spouse (16+) and adult dependants; 60 days after invitation to obtain it, or the application is rejected; exemptions for valid study/work-permit holders who completed Objectif Intégration.
- **Contrat d'autonomie financière:** commit to covering essential needs of self/spouse/dependent children for **3 months from PR**. 2026 barème: 1 adult **$3,957**; 2 adults **$5,803**; 2 adults + 1 child **$6,500** (etc.).
- Valid passport; age 18+; all conditions satisfied at application time.

## 5. French-language requirements in detail

*(Especially relevant if you are preparing the TCF Canada targeting B2 / niveau 7 oral.)*

### 5.1 Required levels (Échelle québécoise des niveaux de compétence en français) — **[official, June 10 materials]**

| Who | Stream | Oral | Written |
|---|---|---|---|
| Principal applicant | Diplômés du Québec | **Level 7** ("intermédiaire avancé") | **Level 5** |
| Principal applicant | Travailleurs étrangers temporaires | **Level 7** | — none |
| Accompanying spouse/common-law partner | Both | **Level 4** | — none |

CEFR mapping (official MIFI correspondence table, version Nov 29, 2024): levels **7–8 = B2**, levels **5–6 = B1**, level **4 = high A2**. So "level 7 oral" = bottom of the B2 band. **[official]**

### 5.2 Accepted tests and diplomas — **[official pre-suspension list / NOT re-published June 10 → carry-over unconfirmed]**

Per the last official PEQ forms (A-0520-GF 2024-11, A-0520-IF 2024-05): **TEFAQ, TCF-Québec, TCF-Canada, TEF-Canada, TCF, TEF, DELF, DALF.** Alternatives to a test:
- Transcripts proving **≥3 years of full-time secondary or post-secondary studies entirely in French** (incl. master's mémoire / doctoral thesis where applicable), or
- Proof of meeting a **Quebec professional order's** French requirements (regular permit valid or expired ≤2 years, or OQLF exam attestation ≤2 years old).

The June 10, 2026 materials list **levels only** — no tests, no scores, no validity. The list above is the pre-suspension framework that MIFI says carries over ("conditions de sélection … restent les mêmes"), with definitive confirmation due **June 17, 2026**. **[official statement of continuity + unconfirmed detail]**

### 5.3 Minimum scores per test (official MIFI correspondence table — program-independent) **[official table]**

**TCF Canada (your test) — to reach oral level 7 (B2 band):**
- **Compréhension orale: ≥ 400** (scale 101–699; band 400–499 = levels 7–8)
- **Expression orale: ≥ 10/20** (band 10–13 = levels 7–8)

**TCF Canada — written level 5 (graduates only):**
- Compréhension écrite: **≥ 300** (band 300–399 = levels 5–6)
- Expression écrite: **≥ 6/20** (band 6–9 = levels 5–6)

**TCF Canada — spouse oral level 4:** compréhension orale **≥ 260**; expression orale **≥ 4/20**.

Same bands apply to TCF and TCF-Québec.

**TEF / TEFAQ / TEF Canada** (all four skills on the 100–699 normalized scale):
- Oral level 7: **≥ 400** in *both* compréhension orale and expression orale
- Written level 5: **≥ 300** in *both* compréhension écrite and expression écrite
- Spouse oral level 4: **≥ 260** in both oral skills

**DELF/DALF:**
- Oral level 7: **DELF B2** with **≥ 12.5/25** in the oral component (any DALF C1/C2 pass exceeds it)
- Written level 5: **DELF B1** with **≥ 12.5/25** written
- Spouse oral level 4: **DELF A2** with **≥ 16/25** oral (an A2 oral score of 12.5–15.5 maps only to level 3 — insufficient)

### 5.4 Validity period and the expired-test grey zone

- Test/diploma results must be **dated two years or less** at the date of application ("datés de deux ans ou moins lors de la présentation de la demande"). **[official, pre-suspension forms + current PSTQ page]**
- **Practical consequence for the first window:** to file between July 2 and October 31, 2026, your test results must be dated no earlier than ~**July 2, 2024** (for the earliest filing) / **October 31, 2024** (for the latest).
- **No accommodation has been announced** for results that expired during the 2024–2026 closure. Advocates (Florence Bollet Michel, "Le Québec c'est nous aussi") flagged expired French tests as one of the persisting "zones grises." This is **officially unresolved**; consultant sites asserting that expired tests will simply be rejected are extrapolating, not citing the ministry. **[unconfirmed]**
- French levels are attested by **signed declaration on the form**, with the ministry reserving the right to verify at an interview and requiring the levels to be **maintained** through processing (otherwise refusal/cancellation possible). **[official, pre-suspension forms]**

## 6. Application process & what happens after

1. **File a demande de sélection permanente in Arrima** starting July 2, 2026, 8:30 a.m. (direct application; no invitation needed for the PEQ). **[official]**
2. Use the **new forms published June 17, 2026** — *"Veuillez ne pas utiliser les anciens formulaires pour présenter votre demande, car elle ne sera pas recevable."* **[official]**
3. **Fees:** spouse/common-law partner **$201** and each dependent child **$201** (2026 rates, non-refundable, indexed each January 1). **[official]** The **principal-applicant fee for the reopened PEQ is not published**; the official 2026 tariff's single "Travailleur qualifié" permanent-selection line is **$940** (up from $921), and the PEQ has historically always used that line — so **$940 is the strong best estimate, but it is a derivation, not a verbatim PEQ statement**. **[official tariff / unconfirmed PEQ application]** Processing starts only once payment is received; personal cheques not accepted.
4. **Incomplete files:** 60 days to supply missing elements, otherwise rejection. **Values attestation:** 60 days for added adults. **[official]**
5. **Processing times:** the MIFI's former **6-month service commitment is explicitly NOT renewed** — *"l'engagement de traiter les demandes dans un délai de six mois … n'est pas reconduit"* — and high volumes over the four-month window may cause longer delays. **[official]** Bonnardel said processing could stretch **over two years** if volumes exceed forecasts **[reported — verbal]**; Moving2Canada expects many 2026 filings to be decided only in **2027** **[reported]**. No prioritization order has been announced. **[official negative]**
6. **After selection:** successful applicants receive a **Certificat de sélection du Québec (CSQ)**, then apply to **IRCC** for permanent residence (federal stage: admissibility, security, medicals). **[official]**
7. **During processing, you are on your own for status:** *"Pendant le traitement de votre demande, vous devrez vous assurer de conserver un permis de travail valide et de le renouveler si nécessaire."* **[official]**

## 7. Transitional measures / who was affected by the freeze

- **The first window itself is the transitional measure** — a "clause de droits acquis" (grandfather clause, as the UMQ calls it) for those blocked by the Nov 19, 2025 abolition. **[official structure / "droits acquis" framing reported]**
- **No new federal work-permit bridging was announced June 10.** Quebec says only that **"discussions are underway with Ottawa"** so permits remain valid until receivable applications are processed. **[reported, La Presse + official MIFI warning]**
- **Pre-existing measures (PSTQ-side, not PEQ):**
  - **IRCC, March 13, 2026:** temporary foreign workers in Quebec with **employer-specific (closed) permits** expiring March 13 – December 31, 2026 who applied/were invited under the PSTQ can get **12-month extensions** (open-permit/PGWP holders **excluded**; deadline December 31, 2026). **[reported]**
  - **Quebec, Arrêté 2026-001 (April 28, 2026, Gazette May 13):** priority PSTQ processing for workers with expired/expiring permits, in effect to May 13, 2027. **[official]**
  - Quebec has asked Ottawa to broaden the federal measure to open-permit holders, spouses and dependants. **[reported]**
- **People who left Canada during the freeze:** Bonnardel said those who met the conditions on Nov 19, 2025 (e.g., now in France or Morocco) **could return to Quebec and apply** — *"pour répondre aux conditions du PEQ … il faut résider au Québec"* — but the required status for returnees (visitor? new permit?) is unspecified. The pre-suspension graduate form accepted "Titulaire d'un visa de visiteur" as a current-stay status, suggesting a possible avenue, **unconfirmed** for the reopened program. **[reported / unconfirmed]**
- **Who falls through the cracks (as announced):** workers who lost their eligible job (fail "occuper un emploi admissible au moment de la demande"); graduates from before ~mid-2023 (fail the 3-years-since-diploma selection condition); people whose French tests expired; near-misses who were days/months short of two years on Nov 19, 2025 (must wait for an undefined later window). **[analysis of official text + advocate-reported grey zones]**

## 8. Context & history

**Shutdown timeline:**
- **Oct 31, 2024** — Diplômés stream suspended (moratorium with the 2025 immigration plan; graduate-stream CSQs had grown from 1,958 in 2022 and 3,643 in 2023 toward ~14,500 expected in 2024). **[official]**
- **June 5, 2025** — Travailleurs stream suspended. **[reported — contemporaneous specialist sources; CIC News says June 25, 2025; no official text cited; June 5 is better corroborated]**
- **Nov 19, 2025** — entire PEQ **abolished** by Décret 1339-2025 (2025 G.O. 2, 6067), repealing RIQ arts. 33–35 under then-minister Jean-François Roberge, as part of the 2026–2029 plan (45,000 admissions/year; ~29,000 economic). Pre-Nov 19 applications continue under the old rules. **[official]**
- **PSTQ** in force since Nov 29, 2024 (replacing the PRTQ); declaration-of-interest/invitation model via Arrima; first rounds from July 2025; ~9,000 invitations in the first four months of 2026. **[official / last figure [reported], single source]**

**Political path:** Fréchette promised revival ~Feb 10, 2026 during the CAQ leadership race → won the leadership **April 12, 2026** (57.9% vs Bernard Drainville), sworn in mid-April → named Bonnardel immigration minister with a relaunch mandate → confirmed in her **May 5** inaugural address → details June 10, two days before the parliamentary session's end (June 12) and ahead of an expected **October 2026 general election**. Pressure included a 17,000+ signature petition, the February 2026 municipal **"Cœurs bleus"** movement, mayors of Montreal and Quebec City, UMQ, FQM, unions, and a Québec solidaire motion. **[reported]**

**Numbers in the plan:** 45,000 permanent admissions/year (2026–2029), incl. ~29,000 economic (plus ~10,000 family reunification, ~6,000 refugees). Bonnardel's target: *"14 500 PSTQ, 14 500 PEQ"* per year — *"Mais je ne suis pas dogmatique … On va s'ajuster selon le flux."* **[plan figures official; split [reported]]** During the first PEQ window, **PSTQ invitations are reduced** and will primarily target people **not** PEQ-eligible (FÉER 4–5 occupations, <2 years' experience); PEQ and PSTQ run **in parallel** through the transition; Quebec aims for roughly equal CSQ counts across the **two PEQ streams**. **[official]**

**Volume disambiguation (important, figures are often conflated):**
- **8,000–12,000 (up to ~14,000)** = ministry estimate of *first-window eligible applicants* **[reported, press conference only]**
- **~14,500/year** = PEQ's intended *annual admissions share* **[reported]**
- **~29,000/year** = the whole *economic-class envelope* **[official plan figure]**
- **~125,000** = the leadership-race-era projection of people a broad acquired-rights clause could cover, now dismissed by Bonnardel as "prévisions à très haut niveau"; he could not explain the gap. **[reported]**
- Denominator context: Statistics Canada counted **514,039 non-permanent residents** in Quebec in Q1 2026, of whom 322,547 held work/study permits. **[reported]**

**Longer arc:** PEQ created February 2010 (Charest government) as a fast track to the CSQ; the 2019 Jolin-Barrette restriction was withdrawn Nov 8, 2019 after outcry. **[unconfirmed — Wikipedia/CBC single-source background]** Historical selections per a QS motion: 5,915 (2022), 9,313 (2023), 20,201 (2024), 18,532 (to Oct 31, 2025) **[reported — single source, in unreconciled tension with other reported series]**.

## 9. Reactions & analysis

**Positive:**
- **UMQ** (Guillaume Tremblay): *"Cette décision arrive à point nommé pour l'ensemble du Québec"* — "we cannot deprive ourselves of people already well integrated, who speak French, who study, work and actively contribute to the vitality of our communities"; calls the measure "une clause de droits acquis claire et efficace." **[reported — UMQ communiqué]**
- **Quebec City mayor Bruno Marchand:** relief — "for the first time in a long time, people settled and integrated here are breathing a sigh of relief" — but implementation must be watched. **[reported]**
- **FIQ** (Julie Bouchard): a positive signal for the ~1,200 affected healthcare professionals. **[reported]**
- **CSN, Manufacturiers et exportateurs du Québec:** welcomed the relaunch. **[reported]**
- **Québec solidaire** (Guillaume Cliche-Rivard): claimed it as *"une victoire de la mobilisation et de Québec solidaire."* **[official QS communiqué, 71026]**

**Mixed / critical:**
- **FCCQ** (Véronique Proulx): "un signal positif" but thresholds remain too low — *"Le Québec a besoin de 106 000 travailleurs étrangers par année"* (advocacy figure, **no methodology cited** — attribute, don't adopt); demands acquired rights for **all** currently employed foreign workers. **[reported]**
- **Les Orphelins du PEQ:** an "insult" and *"a petty political ploy ahead of the October elections"*; demands "permanent, automatic, and quota-free access for ALL those left behind." **[reported]**
- **"Le Québec c'est nous aussi"** (Florence Bollet Michel): grey zones persist — expired French tests, lost jobs, permit renewals — *"Au lieu d'une ouverture pour deux ans franche et claire, on a une ouverture pour quatre mois, avec la promesse potentiellement de nouvelles périodes… Mais les promesses, on en a eu beaucoup."* **[reported]**
- **AQAADI** (Me Yves Martineau): some candidates remain "oubliés"; the government long **overestimated** beneficiary numbers, needlessly fueling anxiety. **[reported]**
- **Conseil du patronat:** warns of a "goulot d'étranglement" (bottleneck) unless admissions of workers already in Quebec rise; **Alliance main-d'œuvre étrangère:** demands transitional status protections. **[reported]**

**Analysis:** the design is politically calibrated — an uncapped window generates goodwill before the October election, while phasing, the Nov 19, 2025 anchor, the job-at-application condition and the unrenewed processing standard keep actual admissions inside the unchanged 29,000/year envelope. The structural tension (uncapped intake vs capped admissions) is resolved by stretching processing — which is precisely why the bridging-permit question, still unsettled with Ottawa, is the biggest practical risk for applicants.

## 10. What remains unconfirmed

**No official source yet exists for:**
1. **The legal instrument.** No regulation re-enacting the PEQ in the RIQ and no ministerial reception-period decision (arrêté under arts. 50/52 LIQ) has been published — Gazette Partie 2 through No. 23 (June 10) searched negative; MIFI decisions page frozen at May 19, 2026. Under art. 104 LIQ such a regulation needs no draft publication and can take effect on Gazette publication — **watch Gazette No. 24 (June 17), No. 25 (June 24) or No. 26 (~July 1)**, the latest lawful slots before July 2.
2. **Principal-applicant fee** for the reopened PEQ ($940 is a derivation from the program-neutral 2026 "Travailleur qualifié" tariff line, not a PEQ-specific statement).
3. **Accepted French-test list and 2-year validity for the reopened program** (pre-suspension framework expected to carry over; due June 17), and **any accommodation for tests that expired during the closure** (flagged grey zone, no answer).
4. **The graduate French-of-studies condition** (≥75% of courses/credits in French OR 3 years of study in French) — omitted from the June 10 lists; probably carries over per the "conditions unchanged" statement, but unconfirmed.
5. **The worker-stream lookback window/anchor** — official text: "at least two years as of Nov 19, 2025," no window; media: "two years within the previous three" anchored at Nov 19, 2025; old regulation: anchored at application date.
6. **Carry-over of the pre-July 22, 2020 grandfathered worker variant** and of the Annexe E exclusions (only in pre-suspension documents).
7. **Status required of returnees** who left Canada during the freeze (visitor status sufficiency unconfirmed).
8. **Communiqué 71024** (Cabinet du ministre) — cited in consolidated research but not located in a dedicated verification pass; only 71021 is verified.
9. **Number, dates and criteria of later intake windows** ("Tout dépend du flux qu'on va recevoir"), and the **possible third-year extension** (single-source ministerial musing that sits in tension with the official "closes permanently July 2, 2028" framing).
